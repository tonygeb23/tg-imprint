Text recognition language data for TG Imprint.

eng.traineddata and spa.traineddata are from the tessdata_fast repository of
the Tesseract OCR project, https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tessdata_fast,
under the Apache License 2.0. They are unmodified. Fetched 2026-11-01.

PyMuPDF carries the Tesseract engine; these are only its language data.
